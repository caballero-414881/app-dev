## Favorite Series/Movie
One of the best series I've watched is **Erased**, or *Boku Dake ga Inai Machi*.
- An animation adapting a manga series of the same title, its story follows an otherwise ordinary man with one special ability: to travel several minutes back in time before tragedy occurs.
- However, an incident occurs one day, and the shock triggers his quirk and sends him 18 years back. 
- Realizing that the incident in the present and an ongoing chain of child kidnappings and serial murder cases in his childhood life are directly connected, he tasks himself with using his intelligence to figure out how to save what's most important to him.

It's not a very profound or unique story, but I really liked the execution and development; I think it's something some shows today should learn from.
